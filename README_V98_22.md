# NOVATEK V98.22 — Smart Compare Full Details

## What changed
- Smart Compare result is now a complete detailed comparison inside the result modal.
- Shows both product images, brand/model, price and full available local specifications.
- Explains why each device wins or loses in CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, screen, battery and price.
- Adds practical winners for gaming, programming/work, design/editing and mobility.
- Shows battery-health data and uses cached external research when a trustworthy battery-life duration is present; it never invents hours.
- Shows external research snippets and source links when available.
- Keeps V98.21 provider-cost protections: SerpApi first, Tavily only as fallback, limited PricesAPI, and Gemini failure does not block the comparison.
- Comparison quota remains 10/month and is consumed only after a successful result.
